What it does

  • Usage limits: block an app or a group of apps after a daily time budget (for example 30 min of social media).
  • Limited opens: allow an app only a few times a day, a few minutes each time.
  • Schedules: block distractions during work, study, meals or sleep.
  • Places: block apps at school, at the office or at home, or everywhere except one place.
  • Focus sessions: block everything distracting for 15 min, 1 h or the time you choose.
  • Websites: block the sites that waste your time in Safari, plus an optional adult content filter.
  • Strict mode: once on, blocks can be tightened but not loosened, and the app cannot be deleted while a block is active.
  • "5 more minutes" from the block screen, capped per day, so a block is a pause, not a prison.
  • Time won back: see how many blocked attempts you resisted and the time it saved.

Why people pick DetoxScreen

Many screen time apps are easy to dismiss or cost a monthly subscription. DetoxScreen relies on Apple's own Screen Time framework (Family Controls), the same system that powers the limits built into iOS, and it is free. It is made by an independent developer in France and available in English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Russian, Turkish, Polish, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.

FAQ

Is DetoxScreen really free?

Yes. Blocking apps is free, with no ads, no account and no subscription.

Can a block be bypassed easily?

DetoxScreen uses Apple's Screen Time technology. With strict mode on, you cannot loosen a block on a whim, and the app cannot be deleted while a block is active.

Which apps can I block?

Any app or category on your iPhone: TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Snapchat, Reddit, games… and websites in Safari.

Does it collect my data?

No account and no personal data. iOS keeps the list of blocked apps on your phone and never shows app names to DetoxScreen. Only anonymous counters are used to improve the app. Privacy policy.

Can parents use it on a child's iPhone?

Yes, on the child's own iPhone: set the blocks, turn on strict mode so they stay in place. It is not a remote parental-control app.

Is there an Android version?

Android is in closed testing for now. DetoxScreen is available on iPhone.
